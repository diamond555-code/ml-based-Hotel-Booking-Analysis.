Key Empirical Findings & Strategic Hotel Operations Roadmap

Based on 119k Booking Analysis
01

The Non-Refundable OTA Paradox

Surprisingly, reservations booked with a Non-Refundable deposit had higher cancellation rates than flexible reservations. Investigation shows this stems from wholesale travel agencies pre-booking blocks that are dumped back when quotas aren't met.

Action: Audit third-party travel distribution channels and enforce strict wholesale cut-off deadlines.
02

Lead Time Risk Thresholds

Bookings made >100 days in advance possess a cancellation probability of nearly 55%, whereas bookings under 30 days cancel under 20% of the time. Long lead times provide an extended window for plan alterations.

Action: Establish an automated email re-engagement cycle at T-60 and T-30 days offering personalized stay add-ons.
03

The Parking Space & Special Request Anchor

Guests requesting a parking space or registering >1 special request exhibited cancellation rates below 12%. These indicate concrete travel logistics (bringing personal vehicles or specific family requirements).

Action: Prompt guests during the post-booking flow to customize room preferences or reserve parking spaces.
